Frost Client v4.1.2

Your key is built into the file, so there's nothing to type in game.

  1. Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API.
  2. Drop the jar in .minecraft/mods and remove any older Frost jar.
  3. Launch the game and press F9.

v3.2.09 Nov 2026
  • Start screen: launching the game opens on your menu sky with a snowflake above the Frost wordmark - click and it glides into the title menu. Can be turned off in Appearance.
  • Butterfly inspect: holding inspect on the butterfly now flicks it up into a smooth display hold, then puts it away with a flick when you let go.
  • Smoother tricks: no more little hop when the inspect settles, and the wooden thud is gone from the knife sounds.
  • Tracker graph: redrawn smooth - anti-aliased line, soft fading fill and a glowing dot on your latest value.
v3.1.08 Nov 2026
  • Profit graph: a stock-style chart of your balance for this stream, today or this week, scaled to each window with money up the side and time along the bottom.
  • Payments tab: every payment in and out with the player's head, name, amount and time - click a name for your full history and PnL with them.
  • Orders tab: delivered orders show the item, its icon and the real loss or gain (your buy price is remembered and subtracted from the payout). Tag outgoing orders with /orders <name>.
  • Follows don't count: when a player you follow buys, sells or pays someone, it no longer moves your total.
  • Safer payouts: a /pay only counts once the chat confirms "You paid …", so a glitched or ignored payment never leaves your total.
  • Knife tricks: a fresh set of random flip tricks, a toss-and-catch throw on every knife, and a flat fan spin.

Rules

  • A key is for you. It locks to your first device. Don't share, sell or resell it.
  • Using a key on more devices than it has slots suspends it and blocks the extra device.
  • Changing PCs? Use your free monthly device reset, or buy another.
  • Need more devices on one key? Add a device slot.
  • Chargebacks or payment fraud permanently revoke the key.
  • Frost Client is a visuals and streaming client. Use it within each server's own rules.
  • Be decent in the Discord. Harassment or leaking keys gets you removed.
