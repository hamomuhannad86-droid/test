Questions

How do I get my key?

Sign in with Discord and pay. Your key is created right away, emailed to you, shown on your dashboard, and your Discord roles are added automatically.

How do I install it?

Download the jar from your dashboard. Your key is already built into the file. Put it in your .minecraft/mods folder with Fabric and Fabric API, launch the game and press F9.

Does it work on any server?

Yes. Every visual is client-side, so it works in singleplayer and on any server. The money tracker reads chat on economy servers, and the Frost-only extras (badges, synced capes and cosmetics) show between Frost users anywhere.

Which Minecraft versions does it support?

Minecraft 1.21.11 with Fabric. Every update lands there first.

What does the device lock do?

The first computer that opens the mod is saved to your license, and the key only works there. To move to a new PC, reset your device from the dashboard: one reset a month is free, extra resets are $3.

Can I switch from monthly to lifetime?

Yes. Buy lifetime from your dashboard and your monthly subscription cancels automatically. Same key, no renewals after that.
